## PROBLEM IDENTIFIED

User requirement: "I need dramatic silhouette disturbance, not subtle. Imagine you see Model A as a black silhouette. What 'signatures' disturb the puffy bell frock they wear?"

**Audit revealed:** 21 of 26 D1_Architectural primaries were just surface decoration - sparkly lines, textured patches, embroidered panels, small projections. These wouldn't show in a black silhouette at all.

**Examples of failed primaries:**
- "Ruffle cascade down bodice" - just decoration ON the dress
- "Crystal diagonal" - sparkly line, doesn't break outline
- "Embroidered flowers" - textured surface, silhouette unchanged
- "Beaded gradient" - sparkly patch, no projection
- "Lace medallion projecting 15mm" - too small to show

**User verdict:** "it's not dramatic... If it won't do that, I don't want it."

---

## SILHOUETTE-BREAKING CRITERIA

**What breaks silhouette (visible as black shadow):**

### SHOULDERS:
- Massive bow explosions extending PAST shoulder line
- Giant ruffle clouds doubling shoulder width
- Wing structures projecting horizontally

### WAIST/HIPS:
- Huge back bow sticking out behind body profile
- Side panniers making waist wider than skirt
- Bustle projections breaking back curve

### VERTICAL:
- Standing collar/ruffle extending ABOVE head
- Trailing elements hanging BELOW hem
- Asymmetric length breaking smooth outline

### RADIAL:
- Cage structures pushing skirt even WIDER
- Peplum creating sharp horizontal break
- Tiered ruffles stacking outward

**What DOESN'T break silhouette:**
- Embroidery, beading, sparkles (surface texture)
- Small projections under 5cm (invisible in shadow)
- Cutwork, lace insertion (transparency, not projection)
- Ribbons laid flat (pattern ON surface)

## KEY LEARNING

**Design principle established:** D1_Architectural primaries must disturb the dress silhouette DRAMATICALLY when viewed as black shadow. If it's just surface decoration (sparkles, texture, embroidery), it's not architectural.

**Test:** Take any primary description. Imagine ModelA as solid black silhouette. Does the embellishment change the outline? If no → delete.

**Scale matters:** 
- 2cm projection = invisible in silhouette → DELETE
- 15cm projection = obvious disturbance → KEEP

**Victorian/Elizabethan inspiration works:** Historical dramatic structures (bustles, panniers, ruffs, cage crinolines) are perfect because they were designed to dramatically alter silhouette.
