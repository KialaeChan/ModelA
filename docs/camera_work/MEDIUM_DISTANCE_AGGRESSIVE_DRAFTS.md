## PATTERN APPLIED

**Aggressive Medium Distance Formula:**

1. **Composition Type Header:** "MEDIUM DISTANCE [TYPE]"
2. **Distance Specification:** "X feet camera distance"
3. **Framing Intent:** "[TYPE] FRAMING:"
4. **Visible Elements:** List what IS in frame
5. **Crop Line:** "Bottom of frame cuts at [specific body location]"
6. **Negative Exclusions:** List 3-5 things NOT visible
7. **Fill Percentage:** "Subject fills X% of frame height"
8. **Detail Emphasis:** What should be sharp/readable
9. **Triple Negative Block:** "NOT full body. NOT distant. NOT complete [something]"
10. **Composition Type Closure:** "[Type] composition only"

## NOTES

- Medium distance is 5-7 feet (between close and full-body)
- Should show more than close-up but NOT complete dress
- Crop line typically mid-thigh to knee
- Still emphasizes face/bodice detail
- Different from full-body (which shows complete silhouette + floor)
