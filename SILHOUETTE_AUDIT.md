## REPLACEMENT STRATEGY

Need ~15-20 new TRUE silhouette-breakers:

**SHOULDER BREAKERS:**
- Wing ruffles extending 20cm+ past shoulders
- Epaulettes standing up above shoulders
- Asymmetric cape/drape over one shoulder

**WAIST/HIP BREAKERS:**
- Bustle structure pushing back curve out
- Side panniers making hips wider
- Peplum creating new horizontal line

**VERTICAL BREAKERS:**
- Standing collar extending above head
- Train extending below hem
- Tall hat integrated into dress

**RADIAL BREAKERS:**
- Cage/hoop structure pushing skirt even wider
- Horizontal ruffle tiers stacking outward
- Asymmetric hemline (short front, long back)
