# MEDIUM PRIMARY TRANSCENDENCE AUDIT
**Goal:** Every primary must BREAK SILHOUETTE, be IMPOSSIBLE TO MISS, create DRAMATIC STATEMENT

---

## EXISTING PRIMARIES (4 original + 1 updated)

### 1. dimensional_lace_overlay_system
**Claims:** "Single large medallion positioned at bodice center... Projects forward from chest creating dimensional relief... ARCHITECTURAL FOCAL POINT breaking flat bodice surface"
**Reality Check:** ❌ FAILS - Subtle lace medallion, doesn't break silhouette, just decorative overlay
**Fix Needed:** Make it MASSIVE, dramatically raised, breaking plane outward

### 2. hand_embroidered_gradient_panels  
**Claims:** "Dense dimensional embroidery covering bodice... Flowers project from fabric surface... TEXTURAL FIELD transforming smooth bodice"
**Reality Check:** ❌ FAILS - Flat embroidery, no silhouette break, texture not dimensional projection
**Fix Needed:** Make embroidery sculptural, petals curl outward from surface, 3D relief

### 3. bow_bonanza_waist_accent
**Claims:** "Large structured bow... Bow loops project forward from waist... dimensional focal point"
**Reality Check:** ⚠️ MARGINAL - Bow at waist can work but needs size emphasis, projection distance
**Fix Needed:** Make it ENORMOUS, loops extend far forward, ribbon tails cascade dramatically

### 4. layered_applique_mixed_technique_system
**Claims:** "Layered appliqué... Beaded elements layered over lace... Each layer adds height building sculptural relief"
**Reality Check:** ❌ FAILS - Mixed techniques read as flat decoration, no silhouette break
**Fix Needed:** Make layers STACK OUTWARD aggressively, each layer projects further than last

### 5. layered_organza_sheer_ruffle
**Claims:** "Multiple tiers of sheer organza ruffles attached at shoulders extending outward... cloud-like volume... breaking the clean shoulder line"
**Reality Check:** ✅ WORKS - Organza at shoulders creates dimensional cloud, extends silhouette
**Keep as is**

## FIX STRATEGY

For AI to understand "breaks silhouette," we need:

1. **Explicit projection distance:** "extends 3-5cm forward from chest"
2. **Comparative language:** "projects FURTHER than sleeve cap," "breaks plane outward past shoulder line"
3. **Physical anchoring:** "attached at base, extends outward," "stems raise elements away from surface"
4. **Shadow casting:** "casts shadow on bodice beneath," "creates gap between element and fabric"
5. **Volume descriptors:** "air space between layers," "dimensional gap visible," "hollowed underneath"

**NOT:** "dimensional," "layered," "textured" - these read as FLAT
**YES:** "projects forward," "extends outward," "stands away from," "breaks plane"
